Building B access card

B I

Collect your card from reception. Try these in order:

  1. Confirm the badge office already has your photo.
  2. Collect from reception on the ground floor of Building B, 8:30–16:00 weekdays.
  3. Temporary stickers from reception cover your first day only.
  4. Ask your manager to confirm the start date if you need access this week.

Still stuck?

Raise a request titled Access card.

Include:

  • Start date
  • Building
  • Manager’s name

If you have never been issued a card, raise Building access.
