Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

OpenRequest is based in the United Kingdom.

This policy covers the marketing site at openrequest.co and the OpenRequest service at app.openrequest.co.

If you have a question or want to use a privacy right, email help@openrequest.co.

Two kinds of data

Site and signup. If you visit the website or create an account, OpenRequest is the controller of that information (for site/account data you give us directly).

Your organisation’s OpenRequest workspace. If your company uses OpenRequest, your organisation is the controller of employee, request, knowledge, and inbox data. We process that data to provide the service, on their instructions.

What we collect

Website visitors

Pages you view, your IP address, and standard server logs, so the site can run and stay secure.

OpenRequest accounts

When an organisation signs up we process names, email addresses, organisation details, branding (icon and logo), teams, request types, requests, conversation threads, knowledge, SLAs, and access settings. Employees sign in through the organisation’s identity provider (SSO). We receive the identity information that provider sends us so we can sign you in.

Email

We send transactional email (invites, verification, product mail) through Resend.

AI

Where AI is turned on, we use workspace content (requests, threads, knowledge, SLAs) to match a request, draft a reply, or watch an SLA. That is to provide the feature, not to sell your data.

We do not sell personal data. On this marketing site we use cookieless analytics via Kobbe after you interact with the page. We do not use analytics in the OpenRequest product.

Why we use it

  • To provide and improve OpenRequest (contract, or steps before a contract)
  • To run the website and keep it secure (legitimate interests)
  • To send product and security email (contract)
  • To meet the law (legal obligation)

Who we share it with

Only what they need to do their job for us:

  • Kobbe — cookieless marketing-site analytics
  • Resend — transactional email
  • Turso — product database
  • Render — hosting
  • Your organisation’s identity provider — SSO
  • AI providers we use to run those features — only workspace content needed for matching, drafting, or SLA watching

We may also share data if the law requires it, or with a buyer if the business is sold, under the same kinds of protection.

How long we keep it

  • Website logs: a short period, unless we need them for security
  • Signup and account data you give us directly: for as long as the account is open, then deleted unless the law says we must keep it
  • Workspace data: for as long as the organisation has an account, then deleted or returned after the account is closed, unless the law says we must keep it

Your rights

If we are the controller (website and signup/account data you give us directly), you can ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to limit or object to our use, and to move it. Email help@openrequest.co.

If your data lives in an organisation’s OpenRequest workspace, ask that organisation. We will help them where we can.

You can complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).

Where it is stored

We are based in the UK. Some processors are outside the UK. Where that happens we use a lawful transfer tool, such as the UK’s standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

Cookies

We use cookies the site needs to work. Marketing-site analytics via Kobbe are cookieless and only load after you interact with the page.

Children

OpenRequest is for organisations at work. It is not aimed at children.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes. The date at the top is the latest version.
